# Privacy Policy — SafeOne

**Last updated: 12 October 2026**

SafeOne ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal safety application published by
**Tejovan Labs** (Seraikela-Kharsawan, Jharkhand, India). This policy explains what data the app uses, how it is used, and
your choices. Contact: **safeeonee@gmail.com**. Please also read our Terms of
Use. SafeOne is offered in India and this policy is written under Indian law,
including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data
Protection Act, 2023.

## Summary

- SafeOne does **not** have a server. We (Tejovan Labs) do **not** collect,
  receive, store, sell or share your personal data.
- All your data (emergency contacts, app-lock PIN, safety log, recordings)
  stays **on your device**.
- The only time information leaves your device is when an SOS, check-in or
  location update is sent — as a text message from **your** phone, directly to
  the emergency contacts **you** chose, or when you open a link or service
  outside the app (such as Google Maps or a phone call). Nothing is ever sent
  to us.

## Information the app uses (all on-device)

**Location.** Used to build a Google Maps link that is included in the SOS /
check-in / "Follow Me" message you send to your contacts. With your permission,
location may be read in the background so the hands-free SOS and safety check-in
can include your location even when the app is closed or the screen is locked.
SafeOne never uploads your location to us or to any server of ours. If you or
your contacts open a Google Maps link, or you use "Nearby help" (which opens
Google Maps), Google receives that information under Google's privacy policy.

**Emergency contacts.** The names and phone numbers you enter are stored locally
on your device (in the app's private storage). They are used only to address your
safety messages. The app does not read your phone's contact list. Because these
are other people's details, please add someone only with their permission.

**SMS messages.** If you allow the SMS permission, SafeOne sends your safety
messages as text messages **directly from your phone** to each emergency contact
you added — one message per contact, using your own mobile plan. It sends only
when you trigger an SOS (including a hands-free trigger), when a safety check-in
runs out without you confirming you're safe, or while live location sharing that
you started is on. SafeOne **never reads or receives** your messages, and message
content is never sent to us. If you don't allow the permission, SafeOne instead
opens your Messages app with the text filled in for you to send.

**Microphone / audio recordings.** If you use the audio-evidence recording
feature, recordings are saved as files on your device only. They are not uploaded.
You can play, share, or delete them yourself. Anything you choose to share
from the app (recordings, the safety log, your location) goes to the app or
person you pick, and is then outside our control. You are responsible for
recording and sharing lawfully and respecting others' privacy.

**Camera.** Used only for features you initiate (e.g. capturing evidence /
QR codes). Captured media stays on your device.

**App-lock PIN and biometrics.** Your PIN is stored only as a salted, hashed
value in the device's secure storage — never as plain text and never transmitted.
Biometric (fingerprint) verification is handled entirely by the Android system;
the app never receives your fingerprint data.

**Safety log.** Records of safety events you log are stored locally and can be
exported or cleared by you at any time.

## Information we do NOT collect

SafeOne contains no analytics, advertising identifiers, crash reporting,
account system, ads or third-party trackers, and we have no servers holding
your data. Separately, Google Play and Android may collect information about
app installs, performance and crashes under Google's own policies; we may see
only aggregated, non-identifying statistics from Google Play.

## Permissions and why they are used

- **SMS (send only)** — send your SOS and location updates directly to your
  emergency contacts.
- **Location (incl. background)** — include your location in safety messages.
- **Microphone** — optional audio-evidence recording.
- **Camera** — optional evidence / QR capture.
- **Foreground service** — keep hands-free SOS triggers active when locked.
- **Exact alarm / full-screen intent / notifications** — deliver the scheduled
  fake call and safety check-in alerts on time, even when the app is closed.
- **Biometric** — unlock the app lock.
- **Boot completed** — restart safety monitoring after a reboot if you enabled it.

## Children

SafeOne is meant for people aged 18 or older. A person under 18 may use it
only with the consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian. We do not
collect data from anyone, including children.

## Security

Your data is protected by your phone's own security and, if you set it, the app
lock. No phone or app is completely secure. Anyone who can unlock your phone
and SafeOne may see your data, so keep your phone lock and app PIN private. We
are not responsible for access to your data through a lost, stolen, shared or
compromised phone.

## Requests from authorities

Because we do not hold your data, we generally have nothing to give if asked.
Your data can only be obtained from your phone itself.

## Deleting your data

Because all data lives only on your device, you are in full control of it and we
hold no copy. You can delete it at any time:

- **Safety log**: open the safety log in the app and tap *Clear all*.
- **Everything**: go to Android *Settings → Apps → SafeOne → Storage → Clear
  storage*, or uninstall SafeOne. This permanently removes your contacts, PIN,
  recordings, settings and log.

If you have any question about your data, email safeeonee@gmail.com and we
will reply within 30 days.
