## 🚫 7 FRAUD TYPES WITH STEP-BY-STEP REPORTING

1. **Romance Scam / Dating Fraud** - Fake romantic interest for money
2. **Fake Job Offer** - Jobs asking upfront fees
3. **Fake Investment Scheme** - Unrealistic return promises
4. **Banking / Phishing Fraud** - Fake bank websites and calls
5. **Online Purchase Fraud** - Fake sellers and non-delivery
6. **Instant Loan Scam** - Fake loan apps with upfront fees
7. **Sextortion / Blackmail** - Extortion using intimate photos

**Each includes:** Steps to report, government portal links, phone numbers
