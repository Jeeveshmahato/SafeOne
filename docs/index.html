Privacy Policy — SafeOne

Last updated: 15 June 2026

SafeOne ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal safety application published by Content Whale. This policy explains what data the app uses, how it is used, and your choices. Contact: product@content-whale.com.

Summary

  • SafeOne does not have a server. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data with us or any third party.
  • All your data (emergency contacts, app-lock PIN, safety log, recordings) stays on your device.
  • The only time information leaves your device is when you send an SOS, check-in, or location message — it goes directly from your own SMS app to the emergency contacts you chose.

Information the app uses (all on-device)

Location. Used to build a Google Maps link included in the SOS / check-in / "Follow Me" message you send. With your permission, location may be read in the background so hands-free SOS and safety check-in can include your location when the app is closed or the screen is locked. Location is never uploaded to us or any server.

Emergency contacts. Names/phone numbers you enter are stored locally and used only to address the SMS messages you choose to send. The app does not read your phone's contact list.

SMS messages. SafeOne does not read, receive, or send SMS silently. Triggering an SOS opens your standard Messages app with the text pre-filled, and you tap "send". We never see the message content.

Microphone / audio recordings. Optional audio-evidence recordings are saved as files on your device only and are not uploaded.

Camera. Used only for features you initiate; captured media stays on your device.

App-lock PIN and biometrics. Your PIN is stored only as a salted, hashed value in the device's secure storage — never plain text, never transmitted. Fingerprint verification is handled entirely by Android; the app never receives your fingerprint data.

Safety log. Stored locally; you can export or clear it anytime.

Information we do NOT collect

No analytics, advertising identifiers, crash data, usage data, account information, or any personal data on our servers. No ads, no third-party trackers.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Location (incl. background) — include your location in safety messages.
  • Microphone — optional audio-evidence recording.
  • Camera — optional evidence / QR capture.
  • Foreground service — keep hands-free SOS triggers active when locked.
  • Exact alarm / full-screen intent / notifications — deliver the scheduled fake call and safety check-in alerts on time, even when the app is closed.
  • Biometric — unlock the app lock.
  • Boot completed — restart safety monitoring after a reboot if you enabled it.

Children

SafeOne is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Data retention and deletion

All data is stored only on your device; delete it any time by clearing it in the app or uninstalling. We hold no copy of your data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change accordingly.
