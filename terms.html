It offers tools such as routines (a timer to reach home that starts when, for example, your office hours end), a siren and flashlight, audio recording, discreet evidence recording (audio, video or photos with the screen dark), a fake call, a medical / emergency ID, safety tips, and lists of helplines and complaint portals. SafeOne works without an account: your data stays on your phone unless you sign in to sync it (see the Privacy Policy).
