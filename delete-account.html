Delete your SafeOne account

SafeOne, by Tejovan Labs, works without an account. You only have one if you signed in with Google under Settings → Account and sync to keep your data the same on several phones.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open SafeOne and go to Settings → Account and sync.
  2. Tap Delete account and cloud data and confirm.
  3. Choose your Google account when asked, and enter your contacts PIN.

Your account and everything synced to it are deleted from the cloud at once. Every phone stops syncing. Each phone keeps the copy it already has, which you can remove with Android Settings → Apps → SafeOne → Storage → Clear storage or by uninstalling SafeOne.

Without the app

Email safeeonee@gmail.com from the Gmail address you signed in with, with the subject Delete my SafeOne account. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and reply to confirm. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your Google name, email address and account ID.
  • Everything synced: emergency contacts, routines, saved trips and SOS settings, and the list of phones signed in.
  • SOS photos uploaded from that phone while you were signed in (if you used photos with an SOS). Any SOS photos are deleted by themselves 7 to 8 days after they were taken anyway.

We keep no copy and make no backups of it. Google, which runs the cloud service for us, removes deleted data from its own systems on its usual schedule (within about 180 days for its internal backups). SafeOne never stores your PINs, recordings, evidence, medical information, safety log or location in the cloud, so there is nothing else to delete. See the privacy policy for details.
